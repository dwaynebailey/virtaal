# I'm Gonna Patch (5000 CVEs)

*An original parody, sung to the rhythm of "I'm Gonna Be (500 Miles)"*

## Verse 1

When the scanner runs at dawn, I know what's waiting there for me:
A queue of fresh advisories, all ranked by severity.
When the pager starts to buzz, I know exactly what I'll see:
Another heap-based overflow in some legacy library.

## Chorus

But I would patch five thousand CVEs,
And I would patch five thousand more,
Just to see the dashboard turning green
And a zero in the score.

## Verse 2

When I'm triaging the feed, I know I'm gonna be
The one who reads the NVD at two a.m. with tea.
When the vendor says "won't fix", I know I'm gonna be
The one who writes the backport and a long apology.

## Chorus

But I would patch five thousand CVEs,
And I would patch five thousand more,
Just to see the dashboard turning green
And a zero in the score.

## Interlude

Re-base, re-build, re-deploy!

## Verse 3

When the auditors arrive, I'll walk them through each SBOM line,
Every transitive dependency, pinned and hashed and signed.
And if a zero-day drops, well, I know I'm gonna be
The one who's up till sunrise, rebuilding the whole tree.

## Verse 4

When the score comes back a ten, I know I'm gonna be
The one who rolls the hotfix out to every last VM.
And when the fix is shipping, I know I'm gonna be
The one who reads the changelog twice, then patches it again.

## Final chorus

So I would scan five thousand repos,
And I would scan five thousand more,
Just to be the one who closed the last
Open port on your front door.
